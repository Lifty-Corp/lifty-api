# Commercial voice

Voice holds general writing preferences shared by every channel, campaign and reply. Read tone and rules before asking. It exists empty at version 0; capture it before the first draft in outreach setup, and from confirmed founder feedback. It is not required to finish the leads interview.

PATCH tone, rules and/or writing_rules with expected_version. Null clears tone, lists replace, omitted fields remain. Rules are do/avoid statements with founder, founder_feedback or migration source. There is no language or example field: the outreach language and, where it has one, formal or informal address are voice rules. Capture them before the first draft, for example `Write every prospect message, including greetings and replies, in Mexican Spanish with usted.` Without one, Lifty writes in the language of the confirmed Business profile.

`writing_rules` is the workspace's own copy of its writing rules, the phrases to avoid that drafting context serves as references.anti_slop: Markdown with `## Global`, `## LinkedIn` and `## Email` sections of `- phrase // optional reason` bullets. Every workspace starts from LIFT's default; the founder edits it freely. PATCH replaces the whole string (at most 20,000 characters; an empty string clears it). It is context, not a check: nothing re-checks copy against it. Change it only when the founder agrees, for example to remove a phrase they want to use. The default list is English: when outreach is in another language, offer to replace it with that language's equivalents.

Follow the workspace's writing rules unless the founder says otherwise. Among founder preferences, campaign-specific instructions win over general voice when they conflict. When the founder corrects a draft, propose whether the correction is general or campaign-specific and save only after confirmation. New drafts read the latest voice; approved active campaigns retain their version pins. Voice never reaches Scout or regenerates research criteria: state a research language in the criteria text.
